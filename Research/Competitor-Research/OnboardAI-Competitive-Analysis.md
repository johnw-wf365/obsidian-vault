## Competitive Gaps (OnboardAI's Openings)

1. **No agent-native onboarding tool for mid-market.** Workday/Leena AI are agentic but enterprise-priced. BambooHR/Rippling are mid-market but not agentic.

2. **No competitor offers inference-aware/hybrid pricing.** Per-seat pricing creates misalignment. OnboardAI's hybrid base+usage aligns cost with customer value.

3. **No competitor deploys in an afternoon.** BambooHR: 2-4 weeks. Rippling: 2-4 weeks + fee. Workday: 4-6 months. Leena AI: 45-90 days.

4. **Mid-market (500-2,000 employees) is overlooked.** Workday targets enterprise. BambooHR targets SMB. Rippling targets breadth. Leena AI targets enterprise.

5. **HR team time recovery is the wedge, not chat.** Competitors lead with "AI chatbot." OnboardAI leads with "reclaim 80% of HR onboarding time."

## Competitive Risks

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|--------|------------|
| Workday/Leena AI drop price for mid-market | Low (12-24 mo) | High | Build workflow depth + HRIS integration moat |
| BambooHR/Rippling add agentic AI | Medium (12-18 mo) | Medium | First-mover advantage; they need architectural change |
| Buyers prefer bundled HRIS + onboarding | Medium | Medium | Position as complement, not replacement |
| AI onboarding commoditized by OpenAI/Anthropic | Low (12-24 mo) | High | Moat = workflow depth + vertical distribution |

## Recommendations

1. **Position against "HRIS with bolted-on AI" not "another chatbot"**
2. **Price Growth tier at $149-$299/mo** (100-500 employees) — current $199/mo is well-calibrated
3. **Lead with time-recovery ROI** — "Reclaim 15-20 hours per new hire"
4. **Integrate with BambooHR and Rippling first** (not Workday) — fastest distribution path
5. **Target 500-2,000 employee segment** as primary beachhead
