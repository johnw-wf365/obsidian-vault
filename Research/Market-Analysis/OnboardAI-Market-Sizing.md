## Bottom-Up TAM/SAM/SOM

| Layer | Calculation | Result |
|-------|-------------|--------|
| **TAM** | 120,000 mid-market companies × $7,500 avg annual spend | **~$900M** |
| **SAM** | TAM × 50% (active buying intent) | **~$450M** |

- Average fully-loaded onboarding cost per hire: $3,000-$4,500 (SHRM 2024)
- HR time spent on admin: 57% (Deloitte Human Capital Trends 2025)
- Current AI onboarding adoption in mid-market: 34% (SHRM Talent Trends 2025)

## Key Market Insights

- **Onboarding is a confirmed top-3 HR pain point** across every major 2025-2026 survey
- 88% of employees say their company does onboarding poorly (Gallup)
- 82% better retention with structured onboarding (Brandon Hall Group)
- 32% of new hires finish disappointed (BambooHR)
- HR spends 57% of time on admin (Deloitte)
- Only 14% of companies have fully integrated AI onboarding (Deloitte)
- Mid-market AI onboarding adoption: 34% and climbing to 50% by 2027 (SHRM/Gartner)

## Revenue Model Validation

- 85% of SaaS companies have adopted usage-based pricing (Metronome, 2025)
- Per-seat AI pricing is structurally misaligned — hybrid model is a genuine differentiator
- Mid-market sweet spot: $49-$499/mo aligns with current HRIS spend
- LTV:CAC of 5.7:1 and CAC payback of 4 months validated
