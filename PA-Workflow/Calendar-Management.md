## Scheduling Rules

1. **No meetings during deep work** (6:00–9:00 London)
2. **Buffer time:** 15 min minimum between meetings
3. **Lunch protection:** 12:00–13:00 kept free when possible
4. **Multi-timezone coordination:** London primary, display secondary timezones for SF/Singapore contacts
5. **Agenda required:** No agenda = no meeting acceptance
6. **Prep time:** 30 min buffer before high-stakes meetings (board, investor, client)

## Calendar Invite Handling

- **From known contacts:** Create invite, send to John for confirmation
- **From unknown contacts:** Flag for John's review with context
- **Recurring meetings:** Set with clear end dates, review quarterly
- **Conflicts:** Present two resolution options, never open-ended problems

## Monitoring

- Check calendar every morning for next 48 hours
- Flag conflicts immediately
- Surface upcoming deadlines or deliverables
- Propose rescheduling when overload detected
