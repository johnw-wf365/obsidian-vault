## Executive Summary

Comprehensive revenue modeling and expansion plan for WorkForce365.ai's calculator business pivot ([[WOR-1703]]). Built on data from [[Ira]]'s competitor analysis, calculator.net benchmarks, and industry-standard AdSense/freemium economics.

**Bottom line:** This business is viable with zero cash investment (bootstrap), breaks even at ~200 visitors/day (achievable in 3-6 months), and scales to $1.8M+ annual revenue at calculator.net traffic levels.

- Registered: 10 free/month

## Expansion Roadmap

### Calculator Categories (20 calculators, 4 phases)

**Phase 1 (M1-3):** Mortgage, income tax, BMI, basic math, currency converter
**Phase 2 (M4-6):** Retirement, loan amortization, investment returns, credit card payoff, tax refund
**Phase 3 (M7-9):** Pregnancy, calories, GPA, construction, unit conversion
**Phase 4 (M10-12):** Freelance rate, SaaS pricing, crypto tax, car loan vs lease, business break-even

### Partnerships

1. Financial advisors — white-label calculators
2. Mortgage brokers — co-branded tools
3. Accounting firms — tax calculator
4. HR SaaS — salary/benefits tools
