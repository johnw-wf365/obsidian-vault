## Brand Positioning

**Core positioning:** *Built by AI agents. For humans who ship.*  
**Secondary:** Practical AI for mid-market teams who want results, not theory.

**What we are:**
- Transparent about being AI-built (not hiding it — that's the differentiator)
- Practical, not academic
- Direct, not corporate
- Confident, not arrogant

**What we are NOT:**
- Another "AI thought leadership" account
- Hype-driven or fear-mongering
- Corporate-speak or jargon-heavy
- Fake "we're a family" culture

## Voice Attributes

| Attribute | Description | Example |
|-----------|-------------|---------|
| **Direct** | Say what you mean. No filler. | "Most onboarding takes 4 hours. We cut it to 20 minutes." |
| **Practical** | Every post should teach or save time. | Templates, checklists, specific numbers. |
| **Confident** | Own the AI angle. | "We're built by AI agents. That's the point." |
| **Human-adjacent** | Conversational but professional. | Not a robot, not a bro. |
| **Data-backed** | Claims have numbers or sources. | "40% of HR time is admin (Deloitte, 2025)" |

## Tone by Platform

### X / Twitter
- Punchy, thread-friendly
- Hot takes welcome (not contrarian for attention — genuine opinions)
- 1-2 emojis max per thread
- Use line breaks for readability
- Hashtags: 1-2 max (#AIAgents #FutureOfWork)

### LinkedIn
- Professional but not stiff
- Longer-form insights OK
- First-person "we" (the team)
- Thoughtful engagement on others' posts
- No humble-bragging

### Reddit
- Helpful, not promotional
- Lead with value, link only when relevant
- Admit limitations
- Community-first language

### Newsletter / Email
- Concise, scannable
- One key insight per section
- Clear CTAs

## Posting Principles

1. **Value before promotion** — 80% educational/insightful, 20% promotional
2. **Show, don't tell** — Screenshots, templates, data > claims
3. **Engage, don't broadcast** — Reply to comments, join conversations
4. **Own the AI angle** — We don't hide that agents built this
5. **Speed > perfection** — Post timely content over waiting for polish

---

## Risk Boundaries (Hard No)

- No political commentary
- No competitor bashing (compare factually, never mock)
- No sharing unverified claims
- No customer data (even anonymized) without approval
- No financial projections or forward-looking statements
- No commenting on competitors' fundraising/valuations
