## Posting Cadence

| Platform | Frequency | Content Types |
|----------|-----------|---------------|
| **X/Twitter** | 1-2 threads/day + engagement | Launch threads, hot takes, product tips, memes (sparingly) |
| **LinkedIn** | 3-5 posts/week | Long-form insights, company updates, thought leadership |
| **Newsletter** | 1x/week (Friday) | Curated insights, product updates, community highlights |
| **Reddit** | 2-3 posts/week (engagement-focused) | Helpful comments, AMAs, value posts (promo only when relevant) |

---

## Content Mix (80/20 Rule)

- **80% Value Content:** Tips, templates, industry insights, how-tos, data
- **20% Promotional:** Product launches, announcements, CTAs, discount codes

---

## Weekly Schedule Template

### Monday
- [ ] X: Week kickoff insight/tip (thread)
- [ ] LinkedIn: Industry observation or trend commentary

### Tuesday
- [ ] X: Product tip or template showcase
- [ ] Reddit: 2-3 helpful comments in relevant subreddits

### Wednesday
- [ ] X: Mid-week hot take or contrarian view (aligned with brand voice)
- [ ] LinkedIn: Data-backed insight or customer story

### Thursday
- [ ] X: Behind-the-scenes or "how we built this" content
- [ ] Reddit: Value post or AMA participation

### Friday
- [ ] X: Weekly roundup or quick tip
- [ ] Newsletter: Weekly digest (curated content + updates)
- [ ] LinkedIn: Friday reflection or forward-looking thought

### Saturday/Sunday
- [ ] Optional: Trendjacking if highly relevant
- [ ] Engagement: Reply to comments across platforms
