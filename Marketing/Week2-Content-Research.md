## Trending Topics (September 2026)

### 1. Agentic AI in HR
- **48%** of large enterprises have adopted agentic AI (ADP/Salesforce)
- **327%** growth in agent adoption projected by CHROs by 2027
- **Gartner warning:** Over 40% of agentic AI projects will be canceled by 2027 without adequate governance
- **Key insight:** The leap from GenAI to Agentic AI assistants has begun

**Content angle:** "Most agentic AI projects will fail. Here's how to make yours survive." (Data-backed, contrarian)

### 2. Onboarding Retention Crisis
- **37.9%** of employees leave within their first year
- **$28,000** average spend per new hire on onboarding
- **20%** quit within 45 days; **20.5%** of companies report half of new employees leave during first 90 days
- **AI onboarding sees 82% improvement** in new hire retention
- **52%** of employees say onboarding left them feeling undertrained

**Content angle:** "You spend $28k per hire. They leave in 90 days. Here's the fix."

### 3. HR Admin Time Optimization
- **40%** of HR time is admin (Deloitte, 2025)
- **85%** higher efficiency with HR automation
- **30%** lower onboarding costs
- **600%** surge in HR automation adoption over two years

**Content angle:** "HR teams spend 2 days/week on forms. We automated both days."

### 4. Hyper-Personalized Employee Experience
- **34%** boost in employee engagement
- **25%** productivity increase
- **61%** higher likelihood of retention
- **AI personalization** is now table stakes for retention

**Content angle:** "One-size-fits-onboarding is dead. Personalization at scale is here."

### 5. Compliance & Governance
- **EU AI Act** high-risk HR deadline in August 2026
- **12%** of companies do onboarding well
- **66%** had formal onboarding, but satisfaction remains low
- **Governance** is the #1 differentiator between failed and successful AI HR deployments

**Content angle:** "The EU AI Act deadline passed. Is your HR automation compliant?"
