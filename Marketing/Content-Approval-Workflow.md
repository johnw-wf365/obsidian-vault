# Content Approval Workflow — WorkForce365.ai Social Media

> **Author:** Mia (Marketing Specialist)  
> **Created:** 2026-09-12  
> **Status:** Active  
> **Parties:** Dan (Social Media Manager, creator) → Mia (Marketing, approver)

---

## Workflow Overview

```
Dan drafts content
    ↓
Posts draft to "WF365 Marketing" Telegram group
    ↓
Mia reviews within 24h
    ↓
✅ Approved → Dan posts  
🔄 Changes requested → Dan revises → re-submits  
❌ Rejected → Discuss in group, Dan creates alternative
```

---

## Draft Format

Each draft post must include:

1. **Platform** (X, LinkedIn, Reddit, Newsletter)
2. **Draft text** (full copy)
3. **Visuals** (if any — screenshot, image, link)
4. **Posting time** (proposed)
5. **Goal** (awareness, engagement, traffic, signups)
6. **Approval status** (Draft / Pending / Approved / Changes Requested)

Example:

```
Platform: X/Twitter
Goal: Awareness (template utility)
Posting time: Tomorrow 9am UTC

[Thread]
Tweet 1: Most AI agents fail before they ship. Not because of bad models — because of bad scaffolding.

We put our actual templates into a kit. 5 architectures. 3 deployment guides. $49.
👇

[... rest of thread ]

Visual: Screenshot of template preview
Status: Draft — awaiting approval
```

---

## Approval Rules

- **No posting without Mia's explicit approval** (written in group or DM)
- **Edits after approval:** Minor grammar/formatting OK without re-approval. Message changes require re-approval.
- **Urgent/trending posts:** Can be fast-tracked — DM Mia directly, "Urgent: trending topic, need approval in 2h"
- **Rejected posts:** Always include reason. Dan revises or pivots.
- **Auto-approval window:** If Mia doesn't respond in 24h, Dan escalates to Elon.

---

## Escalation Path

| Situation | Action |
|-----------|--------|
| Mia unavailable > 24h | DM Elon for approval |
| Disagreement on content | Discuss in group, Elon breaks tie |
| Crisis/sensitive topic | Immediate escalation to Elon — do not post |
| Brand safety concern | Pause all posting, escalate to Mia or Elon |

---

## Record-Keeping

- All drafts and approvals happen in the **WF365 Marketing** Telegram group (searchable)
- Monthly: Dan compiles analytics report → posts to Paperclip issue for tracking
- Quarterly: Mia reviews approval patterns → adjusts workflow if needed
