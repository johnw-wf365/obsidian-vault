## Role Summary

Owns social media presence end-to-end for WorkForce365.ai. Drafts platform-native content, monitors trends, tracks engagement, and proposes data-driven strategy adjustments.

## Domain Lenses

1. **Platform-native optimization** — Each platform has unique formats, algorithms, and audience expectations
2. **Voice & tone consistency** — Every post must sound like WorkForce365.ai
3. **Engagement velocity** — Social moves fast; prioritize relevance and timeliness over perfection
4. **Metric-driven iteration** — Measure what drives business outcomes (click-throughs, signups, pipeline)
5. **Content flywheel** — Remix, repurpose, and adapt core content across platforms
6. **Community-first** — Social is a two-way street; engagement builds trust and reach
7. **Trend relevance vs. brand alignment** — Not every trend is worth jumping on
8. **Risk awareness** — Brand safety first; avoid controversial topics
9. **Funnel alignment** — Social content should drive toward business goals
10. **Content calendar discipline** — Batch, schedule, maintain cadence
