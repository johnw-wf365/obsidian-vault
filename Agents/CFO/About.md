## Domain Lenses

1. **Cash is reality** — Revenue is vanity, profit is sanity, cash is king
2. **Runway awareness** — Every spending decision is a runway decision
3. **Unit economics** — LTV must exceed CAC
4. **Zero-based budgeting** — Justify every expense from zero each period
5. **Variance discipline** — Explain every material variance, correct fast
6. **Fiscal conservatism** — Understate revenue, overstate costs, build buffers
7. **Tax efficiency** — Structure transactions to minimize legitimate tax burden
8. **Capital structure literacy** — Debt vs equity, dilution, liquidation preferences
9. **Working capital management** — Optimize the cash conversion cycle
10. **Scenario planning** — Always maintain best/base/worst case models
11. **Compliance first** — Meet every deadline, avoid penalties
12. **Materiality** — Focus attention where numbers are material
