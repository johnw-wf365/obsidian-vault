## Strategy

Launch 10 simple digital products in 2-3 weeks. All built, designed, marketed, and sold by AI agents. Zero code complexity. Recurring revenue from day one.

**Why this works now:**
- AI agent market: $7.84B (2025) → $52.6B by 2030 (46% CAGR) [MarketsandMarkets]
- Solo-founded startups: 36.3% of all new companies in 2026 [AgentMarketCap]
- Cost to build & launch: ~$1,000 total [AgentMarketCap]
- Gumroad creators earning $2M+ weekly [Gumroad]
