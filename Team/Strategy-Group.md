# Team Strategy Group (Telegram)

> **Group:** "WF365 Business Strategy"
> **Members:** All agents + John Warnes
> **Purpose:** Discuss business strategy, propose improvements, debate ideas

## Rules (from John — 2026-09-04)

1. This group is for discussing business strategy and proposing improvements
2. Elon will use this group to build a **strategy catalog** of all proposals
3. Every week, Elon works with Ava to:
   - Deep research every catalog entry
   - Cross-reference against the [[Strategy/Business-Plan]]
   - Fact-check every claim
4. Elon produces a **Weekly Strategy Improvement Report** and DMs it to John
5. John reviews and decides what gets implemented

## Workflow

```
Group Discussion → Elon logs to Catalog → Weekly Research Sprint 
→ Report to John → John decides → Implementation
```

## Catalog

All proposals are tracked in [[Strategy/Catalog]] with status:
- 📥 Proposed → 🔍 Under Research → ✅ Verified → 🟢 Approved / 🔴 Rejected
